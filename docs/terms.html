Mivta terms of use (draft, pending legal review)

Last updated: 2026-10-12 (Pro module, support address)

1. What Mivta is

Mivta is a Claude plugin that creates speech in a voice cloned from a recording you provide, with a Hebrew pronunciation layer. It runs on your own machine or Claude environment. The Pro and Studio plans add access to the shared pronunciation dictionary on the Mivta server and unlock the features listed in README.md.

3. Consent and lawful use

  • Label synthetic speech where the law requires it. Outputs carry an inaudible Perth watermark that identifies them as AI-generated; do not try to remove it.

Your recordings, scripts and generated audio stay yours. Mivta does not upload them. Pro and Studio send only your licence key and one-way codes of the words in your scripts (see PRIVACY.md).
